1. Conserved-region and phylogenetic analysis of cyanobacterial glucosyltransferases

M.Sc. thesis, SASTRA, Dec 2024. Topic: exopolysaccharide (EPS) biosynthesis

Problem

Glucosyltransferases add sugar monomers to the growing EPS chain, so they shape EPS structure. Which residues are constrained, and does enzyme phylogeny track EPS output?

Pipeline

  • Retrieval: NCBI and UniProt; redundancy removal with CD-HIT at >95% identity
  • MSA: Clustal Omega, BLOSUM62, gap open 10.0, extend 0.1
  • Conservation: Jalview, Shannon entropy per position; BLAST homology check
  • Phylogeny: MEGA Neighbor-Joining, 1000 bootstraps, Poisson-corrected distances

Findings

  • Recurring motifs MILVTVGTE and AHCGEGT across a set spanning 140 to 700+ residues
  • Asp, Glu, His, Cys and Gly consistently conserved; active-site columns fully conserved in Jalview
  • Three tree clusters: Nostoc, Chroococcidiopsis and Cyanobium (high EPS), Synechococcus sp. RSCCF101 (moderate), Pseudanabaena and Leptolyngbya (lower)

Application

Conserved residues are candidate targets for engineering EPS production for food, agriculture and environmental use.

2. SERS detection of dopamine on an electrodeposited copper substrate

M.Sc. thesis, SASTRA, May 2023. Topic: optical biosensing

Problem

Abnormal dopamine levels are linked to neurological disease. Raman scattering is weak (about 1 photon in 10⁵ to 10⁷ is inelastic), so signal enhancement is required for trace detection.

Set-up

  • Copper wire as working electrode; electrodeposition to form the substrate
  • p-aminothiophenol (P-ATP) layer as the SERS probe
  • 1 mM dopamine; spectra recorded 500 to 4000 cm⁻¹

Findings

  • Enhanced peaks observed on the Cu/P-ATP substrate
  • Two dopamine bands read near 1000 and 1500 cm⁻¹
  • Copper is a lower-cost alternative to Au and Ag substrates

Limits

No detection limit or enhancement factor was measured, so no sensitivity is claimed.

3. Salinity response of freshwater and marine Chlorella vulgaris with homology modelling

Project work (BIS500), SASTRA, June 2025. Topic: stress physiology and EPS-linked metabolism

Design

  • Five salinity levels (0 to 100%) by mixing BG-11 and ASN-III, from 100:0 to 0:100; triplicate cultures
  • Readouts: OD 560 nm, chlorophyll (A663), dry weight, Lowry protein (A750), phenol-sulfuric carbohydrate (A490)
  • SWISS-MODEL (ProMod3) for five glycosylation-related proteins; GMQE and QMEAN assessment

Findings

  • Marine strain grew best at 100%; freshwater strain responded late
  • Freshwater carbohydrate: 0.35 (50%, Day 5), 0.65 (75%, Day 5), 0.90 (100%, Day 10)
  • Dry weight stayed near 1.82 to 1.83 g across treatments
  • 50% salinity gave the weakest responses in both strains

Limits

EPS was not directly quantified; carbohydrate is the EPS-linked proxy. Models are moderate confidence (GMQE 0.50 to 0.65).
